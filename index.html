PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Built To Make Racers

Each day teaches one new skill — on the whiteboard and on the simulator. Every lesson builds directly on the one before, so a beginner on Monday is racing wheel-to-wheel in a Lamborghini Huracán GT3 by Friday.

We run a new group every week all summer long, capped at 12 campers so every kid gets real coaching and seat time.

  • Daily whiteboard lesson + coached simulator sessions
  • A new car and a new skill every single day
  • Live championship — points, leaderboards, and a Friday final
  • Friday awards ceremony with a printed certificate
  • All sim-, whiteboard-, and phone-based — no kits or supplies needed

THE CHAMPIONSHIP SYSTEM

Race For The Title

Same format every day — a Time Attack. Drive as many laps as you can; your best clean lap earns championship points. Standings are posted every morning to build the drama all week.

DayCompetition FormatTimePoints
🏆Camp Champion
Highest total points across the four daily Time Attacks plus the Final Race result.
📈Most Improved
Biggest lap-time improvement from Day 1 to Day 5 — rewards effort and growth, not just natural talent.
⚡ No Lead Is Safe

Thursday is Double Points — the Day 4 Time Attack pays 6 / 4 / 2 instead of the usual 3 / 2 / 1. Then the Friday Final pays triple at 9 / 6 / 3. One great lap can flip the entire standings right up to the checkered flag.
