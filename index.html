PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Five Race Cars, One Epic Week

Every day you climb into a different machine and learn the one skill that unlocks it. By Friday you've gone from your very first lap to racing a Lamborghini Huracán GT3 wheel-to-wheel against the rest of the camp.

A fresh group runs every week all summer long, capped at 12 campers — so every kid gets plenty of real coaching, seat time, and racing.

  • Drive 5 different race cars — a new machine every day
  • Go head-to-head against other campers for championship points
  • A live leaderboard, daily drama, and a Friday championship final
  • Awards ceremony with a printed certificate to take home
  • Real coaching on pro-grade racing sims — no experience needed
